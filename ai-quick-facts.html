Tanunda House — Quick Facts

Tanunda House is exclusive-use luxury accommodation in the Barossa Valley, South Australia — a restored 1910 sandstone homestead at 40 Maria Street, in the heart of Tanunda (geo: -34.5264, 138.9577). The whole property is yours; it sleeps 10–13. What sets it apart is shown, not just stated: an outdoor entertaining kitchen with a teppanyaki BBQ and its own outdoor fireplace, a solar-heated pool, a full-size tennis court, an acre of gardens, a theatre room, and five king bedrooms. It suits corporate retreats, milestone celebrations, wine-lovers' weekends, multi-generational family holidays, weddings, and reunions — and because it's in the centre of Tanunda, the group can walk to cellar doors and restaurants without a designated driver. About 1 hour from Adelaide.

Can the whole group really walk to wineries and restaurants?

The Murray Street restaurants and cafés (Musque, Vino Lokal, Darlings, Hustle) are 2–5 minutes. The group can taste, dine, and walk home without a designated driver. Where can a family stay together in the Barossa Valley, including grandparents and grandchildren?

Tanunda House suits a multigenerational family holiday well: the whole family stays under one roof with room to spread out, grandparents get their own bedroom and quiet space, and children have the solar-heated pool, the tennis court, an acre of gardens and a theatre room. How do we visit wineries that aren't walkable?

Everything beyond the two walkable cellar doors is a 10–20 minute drive. Wine-tour operators (Barossa Cruisin, All Barossa Tours, Barossa and Beyond) pick up from the door, so no one has to drive. You can also hire bikes from Barossa Cycle Hub, one minute away.

Is the pool heated?

Yes, it's solar heated — warmest and most used in summer and spring, and cooler in winter. There's no lifeguard, so guests supervise their own group and children.
